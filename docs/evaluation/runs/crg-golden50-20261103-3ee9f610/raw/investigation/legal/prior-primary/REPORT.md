Four deterministic checks found the exact whitespace-normalized clauses in both original and consolidated primary files:

- Article24(3): no probation for a labour contract with a term **under one month**. The rule is not phrased as one month or less.
- Article25: only one probation for one job. Article25(2) gives **60 days** for a job whose professional/technical title requires college qualification or higher; this is not a universal probation limit. - Article27: employer gives the outcome at the end; during probation either party can cancel the probation/labour contract without notice or compensation under that clause.

## Imported scrap: licence subject and authority must be kept separate

[72/2020/QH14](https://chinhphu.vn/default.aspx?docid=202613&pageid=27160) original41 uses the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment and is historical text; its general commencement is2022-01-01 under170. [146/2025/QH15](https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/van-ban/luat-so-146-2025-qh15-468706.htm) changes that contract from2026-01-01:

- Article1(9) replaces39(1),(3): imported scrap as production input or hazardous-waste-treatment service is an alternative licence trigger, separate from the emissions limb. It retains the statutory exceptions, including emergency public-investment projects and exemptions provided by law/NA resolution. A claim of no emissions alone therefore does not establish exemption.
- Article1(11) replaces41. Ministry of Agriculture and Environment covers imported-scrap establishments in41(1)(b).41(2) reserves nationaldefence/security state-secret projects/establishments for the corresponding ministries. Provincial-chair authority in41(3) is residual.
- Article16 supplies commencement; its waterplanning/fisheries delayed clauses do not defer these replacements. Article17 preserves earlier rules/procedures until replacement, existing permits until their term, and pending files under receipt-time rules unless the applicant requests the new law. The administrative chain cannot be flattened to a single date/name substitution.
